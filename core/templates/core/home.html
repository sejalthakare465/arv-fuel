ARV Fuel

"Fueling the body to fight, one meal at a time."

Living with HIV requires more than just medicine—it requires a precise nutritional foundation. ARV Fuel bridges the gap between you and your nutritionist to ensure your body is strong enough to make the most of your Antiretroviral therapy.

What is ARV Fuel?

This isn't a cold clinical tool. It's a digital health journal. We help you track your fats, calories, and proteins through visual sketches, remind you of your medication times, and let your doctor see your progress live through your meal selfies.

Every meal counts toward your strength.
